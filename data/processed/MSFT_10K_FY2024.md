## ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Microsoft Corporation is a technology company whose mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. We develop and support software, services, devices, and solutions that deliver new value for customers and help our clients realize their full potential in an era of intelligent cloud and artificial intelligence.

### Operating Segments
We operate our business across three core segments:
1. **Productivity and Business Processes:** Includes Office Commercial (Microsoft 365 subscriptions), Office Consumer, LinkedIn, and Dynamics business solutions (ERP and CRM).
2. **Intelligent Cloud:** Consists of our public, private, and hybrid server products and cloud services, most notably Microsoft Azure, Windows Server, SQL Server, Visual Studio, and Enterprise Services.
3. **More Personal Computing:** Includes Windows OEM licensing, Surface devices, Xbox gaming hardware and content (including Activision Blizzard integration), and Search and news advertising.

## ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

### Cloud Infrastructure & AI Capacity Constraints
Our business model increasingly relies on massive, hyperscale cloud data centers to support customer migrations to Azure and the rapid deployment of generative AI services (including Copilot and Azure OpenAI Service). If our supply chain partners experience delays delivering custom AI accelerators, electrical power infrastructure, or specialized server racks, we may face capacity constraints that prevent us from fulfilling customer demand, resulting in lost market share and delayed revenue recognition.

### OpenAI Partnership & Technological Dependencies
We maintain significant commercial partnerships and equity investments in OpenAI. We license OpenAI proprietary foundation models to power Microsoft Copilot across our commercial ecosystem. Any impairment in OpenAI's operational execution, internal governance disputes, intellectual property copyright litigation, or divergence in technological roadmaps could impact our competitive positioning in enterprise AI.
