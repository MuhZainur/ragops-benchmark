## ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. Founded in 1977, the Company offers products including iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, and Apple Vision Pro.

### Ecosystem & Services
The Company's business strategy leverages its unique ability to design and develop its own operating systems (iOS, macOS, watchOS, iPadOS, visionOS), hardware, application software, and services to provide its customers products and solutions with innovative design, superior ease-of-use, and seamless integration. Services include the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, AppleCare, Apple TV+, and iCloud storage subscriptions.

---

## ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

### Regulatory and Antitrust Scrutiny on the App Store
The Company faces intense legal and regulatory scrutiny across the United States, European Union, and other international jurisdictions regarding its App Store guidelines, digital commissions, and developer terms. In March 2024, the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), along with 16 state attorneys general, filed a civil antitrust lawsuit alleging Apple illegally monopolizes smartphone markets under Section 2 of the Sherman Act. Additionally, compliance with the European Union's Digital Markets Act (DMA) requires Apple to permit alternative app marketplaces and payment processors, which may reduce Services gross margin and commission retention.

### Global Supply Chain and Geopolitical Exposure
A substantial portion of the Company's manufacturing, final assembly, and component sourcing is performed by outsourced partners located in China, India, and Vietnam. Any disruption caused by natural disasters, trade tariff escalations, or regional conflicts could adversely affect the Company's product delivery timelines and cost structure.
